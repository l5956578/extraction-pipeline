## Book purpose (one paragraph)

CEFR 2001 is the **foundational metalanguage** for European language education: action-oriented approach, plurilingualism, common reference levels A1–C2, illustrative descriptor scales for communicative activities/strategies/competences, and extensive guidance on curriculum, teaching, and assessment. Companion 2020 **does not replace** this book’s full descriptive scheme and policy chapters; it **updates descriptors** and **deepens** mediation, online use, plurilingual/pluricultural, phonology, and signing.

## Chapter 4 — Language use and the language user/learner (~52–…)

**Intent:** The **descriptive scheme in depth** — contexts, communication themes, tasks, texts, strategies, and activity categories. This is where 2001 remains **richest** for situation design.

Notable anchors Companion leans on:
- **4.1.1 Domains** → Companion App 5 “Situation (and roles)” columns
- Communicative activities taxonomy → Companion Ch 3 structure
- Themes / situations inventories → lesson context design (often under-used if only Companion scales are grepped)

**When primary:** Building *situations*, themes, text-types, and task parameters; not just picking a can-do line.
