## Book purpose (one paragraph)

The Companion **updates and extends** CEFR 2001: modality-inclusive / gender-neutral descriptors; expanded reception/production/interaction; **mediation** as a full fourth activity mode; **online interaction**; **plurilingual/pluricultural** scales; **phonology** redesign; **signing competences**; domain examples (App 5); validation methodology (App 6); and explicit **2001 wording changes** (App 7). It repeatedly points back to CEFR 2001 chapters so readers can hold both books together.

## CHAPTER 3 — Communicative language activities & strategies (~47–122)

**Intent:** The **operational storehouse** of can-do scales for activities (what people *do* with language).

### 3.1 Reception (~47–59)
- **Activities:** oral comprehension, conversation between others, live audience, announcements, AV media, reading subtypes, etc.
- **Strategies:** identifying cues / inferring.
- **Prose utility:** Progression notes (“Progression up the scale…”) teach *how* levels differ, not just list cans.

### 3.2 Production (~60–69)
- Sustained monologue, addressing audiences; written production (creative writing, reports).
- Strategies: planning, compensating, monitoring/repair.

### 3.3 Interaction (~70–89)
- Oral interaction scales; written interaction; **online conversation**; **goal-oriented online transactions**.
- Strategies: turntaking, cooperating, asking for clarification.
- **Companion-distinct:** online scales are not in 2001 as such.

### 3.4 Mediation (~90–122)
- **Activities:** overall mediation; mediating a text (relay, explain data, process, translate, notes, creative-text response/analysis); mediating concepts (collaborate, lead, manage interaction, conceptual talk); mediating communication (pluricultural space, intermediary, delicate situations).
- **Strategies:** explain new concept; simplify text (amplify / streamline).
- **Prose utility:** Defines Language A/B, relational vs cognitive mediation — essential coaching vocabulary.
- **2001:** mediation existed mainly as interpreting/translating sketch; Companion is primary for teaching mediation today.

**Product tier:** mostly `assessment_action` + `detailed` on scales.
