## Book purpose (one paragraph)

Threshold 1990 is a **detailed communicative objective specification** for adult learners of English who need to operate with reasonable independence abroad and in social contact with people from other countries. It is **not a syllabus or course**. It lists **language functions**, **general notions**, **specific notions (themes)**, discourse patterns, text skills, writing, sociocultural competence, compensation strategies, learning-to-learn, and degree of skill — with recommended **exponents** (formulae and freer wordings). It is the **higher, freer** objective in the van Ek–Trim pair; **Waystage 1990** is the deliberately lighter station on the same model.

**Language functions — six broad categories (ch. 7):**
1. Imparting and seeking factual information  
2. Expressing and finding out attitudes (factual agreement/knowledge/modality; volitional; emotional; moral)  
3. Getting things done (suasion)  
4. Socialising  
5. Structuring discourse  
6. Communication repair

## Agent navigation tips (Threshold)

1. **Need situations / transactions** → extended characterisation (ch. 5) + specific notions (ch. 9).  
2. **Need “what can they *do* with language?”** → language functions (ch. 7), not CEFR level labels alone.  
3. **Need dialogue flow** → verbal exchange patterns (ch. 10).  
4. **Need sound/intonation** → Appendix A **visually** (PNG/Vision), not text dump.  
5. **Relation to CEFR** → Treat as **B1-oriented objective inventory** feeding CEFR 2001 / Companion can-dos, not as a substitute for them.  
6. **Relation to Waystage** → Same model, heavier freer range; see `RELATIONSHIP_THRESHOLD_WAYSTAGE.md`.
