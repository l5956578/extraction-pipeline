#### 8 Deixis

Deixis involves referring or identifying by means of linguistic items belonging to closed sets the reference of which is dependent upon the context of the utterance (e.g. time, place, persons involved). Deixis may be definite or indefinite (*he* vs. *someone*), non-anaphoric or anaphoric (i.e. referring to an item already mentioned) (ˈWhy don't you ˎcome? vs. I'll ˈbuy those ˎbooks | because I ˎneed them.)
