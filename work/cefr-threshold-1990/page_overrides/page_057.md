**3.5** *earliness*  
**early** (as in: You are ˈearly; There's an ˈearly ·train on ˎMondays.)

**3.6** *lateness*  
**late** (as in: We'll ·have to ˈhurry, | we are ˈlate; We were ˈtoo ·late for the ˎtrain; We ·went to the ˎlate ·show.)

**3.7** *anteriority*  
present perfect (as in: I ˈhaven't ˈseen ·John | ˎyet; I've ·been to ˎParis.)  
past perfect (as in: I ˈhadn't ˎdone it.)  
before + NP/sub-clause  
**before** (as in: I'd ˈnever ˎdone it before.)  
**already** (as in: I have alˈready ˎdone it.)  
**yet** (as in: ˈHas he ˎcome ·yet?; He ˈhasn't ˎcome ·yet.)  
earlier than ...

**3.8** *posteriority*  
after + NP/sub-clause  
afterwards, later (on)  
later than ...

**3.9** *sequence*  
**first** (as in: ˈFirst we ·went to Maˎdrid.), **then** (as in: ˈFirst we ·went to Maˎdrid, | ˈthen we ·travelled to Gibˎraltar.), **next** ● (as in: ˈWhat did you ·do ˎnext?), **finally** (as in: ˇFinally | we ·went ˎback.), later on ●, in the end ●, afterwards

**3.10** *simultaneousness*  
when + sub-clause ●, while + sub-clause ●, as soon as + sub-clause ●  
at the same time

**3.11** *future reference*  
NP + be going to ...  
NP + will ...  
present continuous of verbs of motion  
simple present (with adverbials of future time)  
(as in: We ˈleave at ˎmidnight.)  
**soon**  
**in** (as in: in ˈfour ˎweeks)  
next week/month/year/Sunday, etc.  
tonight, tomorrow, the day after tomorrow, this afternoon

**3.12** *present reference*  
present continuous  
simple present  
present perfect  
at present, now, today, still, (as in: He is ˈstill ˎworking.)  
this morning/afternoon/year etc.

**3.13** *past reference*  
past continuous  
simple past  
yesterday, the day before yesterday, formerly, just, recently ●, lately ●, last week/month, etc.

**3.14** *reference without time focus*  
simple present (as in: ˈEdinburgh is in ˎScotland.)

**3.15** *delay*  
**later** (as in: The ˈtrain will come ˎlater.)  
**delay** ● (as in: There will be a deˎlay.)  
to be delayed ● (as in: The ˈtrain will be deˎlayed.)

**3.16** *speed*  
**fast**, **slow**  
... miles/kilometres per hour

**3.17** *frequency*  
always, (hardly) ever, never, (not) often, once, rarely, seldom ●, sometimes, twice,
