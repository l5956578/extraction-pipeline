### 6 Communication repair

(See also Chapter 12: Compensation Strategies.)

##### 6.1 signalling non-understanding

**6.1.1** 'Sorry, I 'don't underˌstand.

##### 6.2 asking for repetition of sentence

**6.2.1** (I 'beg your) 'pardon?

**6.2.2** 'What did you ˌsay ˌplease?

**6.2.3** ('Sorry) 'could you ˌsay that aˌgain (ˌplease)?

**6.2.4** 'Could you reˌpeat that ˌplease?

##### 6.3 asking for a repetition of a word or phrase

**6.3.1** (sorry +) *wh* question  
'Sorry, 'where does she ˌlive?

**6.3.2** (sorry +) *wh* did you say + interrogative clause  
'Sorry, 'what did you ˌsay his ˌname was?

##### 6.4 asking for confirmation of text

**6.4.1** Did you say: X?  
'Did you say ˌanchovies?

##### 6.5 asking for confirmation or understanding

**6.5.1** Do you mean to say + *that* clause?  
'Do you ˌmean to ˌsay that they ˌaren't ˌcoming?

##### 6.6 asking for clarification

**6.6.1** (Sorry) What does X mean?  
'What does ˌanglophile ˌmean?

**6.6.2** What do you mean by X?  
'What do you ˌmean by ˌgood?
