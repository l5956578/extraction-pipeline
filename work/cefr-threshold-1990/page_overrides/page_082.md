## 9 Shopping

The learners can use shopping facilities, particularly obtaining foodstuffs, clothes, household articles and smokers' requisites, discuss prices, pay for things bought; exchange information and views on these matters. For each of the following sub-themes, see also under General Notions, particularly 2.8.1, 5.1.1, 5.1.8–9, 5.1.14, 5.2.1–2.
