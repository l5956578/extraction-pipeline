##### 2.27 expressing preference

**2.27.1** I('d) prefer + NP | to + VPinf  
I'd pre'fer to ˌgo by ˌtrain.

**2.27.2** I prefer + NP + to + NP  
I pre'fer 'hockey | to ˌfootball.

**2.27.3** I('d) rather (not) + VP  
I'd 'rather ˌnot ˌfly ˌthere.

**2.27.4** I('d) rather VPinf (*than* + contrastive element)  
I'd 'rather ˌdrink ˌcoffee | than ˌtea.

##### 2.28 enquiring about preference

**2.28.1** interrogative sentences and *wh* questions corresponding to 2.27  
'Do you preˌfer ˌcoffee to ˌtea?  
'Which do you preˌfer?  
'Where would you ˌrather ˌgo, | 'London or ˌOxford?

**2.28.2** NP or NP?  
'Tea or ˌcoffee?

**2.28.3** Adj or Adj?
