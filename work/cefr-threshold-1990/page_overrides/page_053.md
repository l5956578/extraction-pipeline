##### 6.15 spelling out a word or expression

**6.15.1** spelling out English letter names  
M- O- N- K- E- Y- S

**6.15.2** X is spelt: ...  
Tough is spelt T- O- U- G- H.

**6.15.3** You spell it: ...  
You spell it B- R- I- G- H- T.

**6.16.3** I think you mean X?
