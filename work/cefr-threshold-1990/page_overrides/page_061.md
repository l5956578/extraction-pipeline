#### 6 Mental

**6.1** *reflection*  
to believe, to be sure, to be certain ●, to hope, to know, to remember, to think, to wonder ●  
no doubt ●  
See also Language Functions 2.5–2.8, 2.13–2.14.

**6.2** *expression*  
to answer, to apologise ●, to ask, to forbid ●, to invite ●, to laugh, to recommend ●, to request ● (as in: ·Guests are reˎquested to ˈleave their ·keys at the ˎdesk.), to say, to speak, to talk, to tell, to thank, to write  
nouns: answer, question, statement

#### 7 Relational

**7.1** *spatial relations*

**7.2** *temporal relations*

**7.3** *action/event relations*

**7.3.1** *agency*  
agent as subject  
agent in by adjunct (passive) ●  
agent in emphatic: It was X who ... ●

**7.3.2** *objective/factitive*  
objective as object (as in: ˈJohn ·opened the ˎdoor.)  
objective as subject of passive ● (as in: The ˈdoor was ·opened by ˎJohn.)  
factitive as object (as in: She ·made a ˈnew ˎdress.)  
factitive as subject of passive ● (as in: ˈThis caˎthedral | was ·built in the ˈthirteenth ˎcentury.)

**7.3.3** *dative*  
dative as indirect object (as in: He ˈgave me a ˎbook.)  
dative in to adjunct (as in: He ˈgave the ·ticket to my ˎbrother.)  
dative as subject of passive ● (as in: He was ·given a ˎbook.)

**7.3.4** *instrumental*  
instrumental in with adjunct (as in: You can ˈopen the ·door with ·this ˎkey.)

**7.3.5** *benefactive*  
benefactive in for adjunct (as in: I have ˈbought this for my ˎwife.)

**7.3.6** *causative*  
to have (as in: ˈCan I have my ˎshirt ·washed, ·please?)

**7.3.7** *place*  
See General Notions, Section 2.

**7.3.8** *time*  
See General Notions, Section 3.

**7.3.9** *manner, means*  
in this way, like this ●  
by means of ... ●  
by + Ving  
as ● (as in: They ˈuse it as a ˎfork.)  
adverbs: badly, fast, hard (as in: We ·have to ·work ˈvery ˎhard.), how?, quickly, slowly, well
