##### 1.3.2 Use

At Threshold Level, ability to use the genitive productively is required only with nouns denoting persons or animals.

In other cases, of + NP may be used. The learners will be able to understand also such phrases as: a day's work, our country’s Prime Minister.

Learners should be able to use the genitive to express the possessive relation (6.7.5.1), including ownership, but also other relationships such as parts of the body, kinship, social roles, mental and physical attributes and activities, etc.

Mary’s feet, John’s mother, the secretary's boss, the actor's performance, etc.
