### 4.3 Communicative tasks and purposes

#### 4.3.1

Acts of communication with one or more interlocutors are generally undertaken by a language user in pursuance of his or her needs in a given situation. In the personal domain, the intention may be to entertain a visitor by exchanging information on families, friends, likes and dislikes, to compare experiences and attitudes, etc. In the public domain, it will usually be to transact business, say to buy clothes of good quality at a reasonable price. In the occupational domain, it may be to understand new regulations and their implications for a client. In the educational domain it may be to contribute to a roleplay or a seminar, or write a paper on a specialised topic for a conference or for publication, etc.

#### 4.3.2

Over the years, needs analyses and language audits have produced an extensive literature on the language-using tasks a learner may be equipped or required to tackle in order to deal with the exigencies of the situations which arise in the various domains.
