> **Users of the Framework may wish to consider and where appropriate state:**
>
> - *what discourse features the learner is equipped/required to control; which macrofunctions the learner is equipped/required to produce; which microfunctions the learner is equipped/required to produce; what interaction schemata are needed by/required of the learner; which he/she is assumed to control and which are to be taught; according to what principles macroand microfunctions are selected and ordered; how qualitative progress in the pragmatic component can be characterised.*
