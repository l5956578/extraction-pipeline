### 8 Deixis

Deixis is the grammatical system for referring or identifying by means of linguistic items belonging to closed sets the reference of which is dependent upon the context of the utterance (e.g. time, place, persons involved). Deixis may be definite or indefinite (*he* vs. *someone*), non-anaphoric or anaphoric (i.e. referring to something mentioned before) (*Why don't you come?* vs. *I'll buy those books because I need them.*).
